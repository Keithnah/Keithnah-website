Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

EnglishBahasa Malaysia

In short

  • We collect the details you give us in the Quick Enquiry form, and a record of which WhatsApp button you clicked.
  • We use them only to answer your enquiry and help you with property.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by WhatsApp or email.

1. Who we are

This website, keithnah.com, is run by Keith Nah under the name Keith Nah Real Estate ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal data in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), as amended.

2. What we collect

WhenWhat we collect
You send the Quick Enquiry formYour name, WhatsApp number and country code, email address (optional), preferred area and what you are looking for.
You click a WhatsApp buttonWhich page and button you clicked, the pre-filled message, your language setting, your device type (mobile, tablet or desktop), the website you came from and the time. We do not collect your name or phone number at this point.
You message or call usYour WhatsApp name and number, email address and whatever you choose to tell us in the conversation.
You browse the siteYour language and currency choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is not sent to us. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.
Site statisticsWe use Google Analytics to see how the site is used: pages viewed, the language version, approximate country and city, device and browser type, how you found the site, and when you click WhatsApp or send the enquiry form. Google Analytics uses cookies for this. It does not receive your name, phone number or email address, and we do not use it for advertising. We also use Microsoft Clarity to see, in summary, where visitors click and scroll (heatmaps and anonymised session replays), so we can make pages easier to use. Clarity hides what you type into forms, uses cookies, and is not used for advertising.

We do not use advertising cookies. You can block analytics cookies (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity) in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), and the site will still work. We do not ask for sensitive personal data such as health, religion or political views, and please do not send it to us.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you the property information you asked for.
  • To arrange viewings, share prices, floor plans and availability, and follow up with you.
  • To understand which pages and projects people are interested in, so we can improve the website.
  • To meet legal and regulatory requirements, where they apply.

We only send you new project updates if you have asked for them or agreed to receive them. You can stop them at any time by replying "STOP" or telling us.

4. Your consent

By sending the Quick Enquiry form or clicking a WhatsApp button on this website, you agree to us using your personal data as described here. Giving us your details is voluntary, but without your contact details we cannot reply to your enquiry. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 8).

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We only share it with:

  • Service providers that run this website for us: Hostinger (website hosting), Google (Google Sheets, where enquiries are stored, and Google Analytics, for site statistics), Microsoft (Clarity, for heatmaps), Telegram (notifications to Keith's phone) and WhatsApp (Meta), when you message us.
  • Developers, banks, lawyers or other parties involved in a purchase, only when you ask us to take your enquiry further, and only the details needed.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Malaysia. Where that happens, we rely on them to protect your data to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as we are helping you, and for up to 2 years after our last contact, unless a longer period is required by law or you become a client. After that, we delete them.

7. How we protect it

Enquiry records are stored in a private Google account that only Keith can access. The website is served over a secure HTTPS connection. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong, incomplete or out of date;
  • stop using your data for marketing, or limit how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent and delete your data, unless we must keep it by law.

Send your request by WhatsApp or email (details below). We will reply within 21 days. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act on a request.

9. Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, such as developers' websites, news articles, YouTube and WhatsApp. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

10. Children

This website is meant for adults looking for property. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date at the top.

12. Contact us

For any question or request about your personal data:

Keith Nah, Keith Nah Real Estate
Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
WhatsApp: +60 10-282 1568
Email: njh0308@hotmail.com

If the English and Bahasa Malaysia versions differ, the English version applies.

Notis Privasi

Berkuat kuasa 12 November 2026

Ringkasan

  • Kami mengumpul maklumat yang anda berikan dalam borang Quick Enquiry, dan rekod butang WhatsApp yang anda klik.
  • Kami menggunakannya hanya untuk menjawab pertanyaan anda dan membantu anda berkaitan hartanah.
  • Kami tidak sekali-kali menjual data peribadi anda.
  • Anda boleh meminta untuk melihat, membetulkan atau memadam data anda pada bila-bila masa melalui WhatsApp atau e-mel.

1. Siapa kami

Laman web keithnah.com dikendalikan oleh Keith Nah di bawah nama Keith Nah Real Estate ("kami"). Notis ini menerangkan cara kami mengendalikan data peribadi selaras dengan Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 (PDPA) Malaysia, sebagaimana yang dipinda.

2. Data yang kami kumpul

  • Borang Quick Enquiry: nama, nombor WhatsApp dan kod negara, alamat e-mel (pilihan), kawasan pilihan dan jenis hartanah yang dicari.
  • Apabila anda klik butang WhatsApp: halaman dan butang yang diklik, mesej yang telah diisi, tetapan bahasa, jenis peranti, laman web asal dan masa. Kami tidak mengumpul nama atau nombor telefon anda pada peringkat ini.
  • Apabila anda menghubungi kami: nama dan nombor WhatsApp, alamat e-mel dan apa sahaja yang anda kongsi dalam perbualan.
  • Semasa melayari laman web: pilihan bahasa dan mata wang disimpan dalam pelayar anda sendiri dan tidak dihantar kepada kami. Penyedia hos kami mungkin menyimpan log pelayan biasa (seperti alamat IP dan jenis pelayar) untuk tujuan keselamatan.

Kami menggunakan Google Analytics untuk statistik laman web (halaman yang dilihat, versi bahasa, anggaran negara dan bandar, jenis peranti, cara anda menemui laman ini, dan klik WhatsApp atau borang pertanyaan). Ia menggunakan kuki, tetapi tidak menerima nama, nombor telefon atau e-mel anda, dan tidak digunakan untuk pengiklanan. Kami juga menggunakan Microsoft Clarity untuk melihat secara ringkasan di mana pelawat klik dan tatal (peta haba dan rakaman sesi tanpa nama) supaya halaman lebih mudah digunakan. Clarity menyembunyikan apa yang anda taip dalam borang, menggunakan kuki dan tidak digunakan untuk pengiklanan. Anda boleh menyekat kuki analitik dalam tetapan pelayar anda. Kami tidak menggunakan kuki pengiklanan dan tidak meminta data peribadi sensitif.

3. Tujuan penggunaan

  • Menjawab pertanyaan anda dan menghantar maklumat hartanah yang diminta.
  • Mengatur lawatan, berkongsi harga, pelan lantai dan ketersediaan unit, serta membuat susulan.
  • Memahami halaman dan projek yang diminati untuk menambah baik laman web.
  • Mematuhi keperluan undang-undang dan peraturan, jika berkenaan.

Kami hanya menghantar maklumat projek baharu jika anda memintanya atau bersetuju menerimanya. Anda boleh berhenti pada bila-bila masa dengan membalas "STOP".

4. Persetujuan anda

Dengan menghantar borang Quick Enquiry atau mengklik butang WhatsApp di laman web ini, anda bersetuju dengan penggunaan data peribadi anda seperti yang diterangkan di sini. Pemberian maklumat adalah secara sukarela, tetapi tanpa butiran hubungan anda, kami tidak dapat menjawab pertanyaan anda. Anda boleh menarik balik persetujuan pada bila-bila masa.

5. Pihak yang menerima data

Kami tidak menjual atau menyewakan data peribadi anda. Kami hanya berkongsi dengan:

  • Penyedia perkhidmatan laman web kami: Hostinger (hos laman web), Google (Google Sheets, tempat pertanyaan disimpan, dan Google Analytics, untuk statistik laman web), Microsoft (Clarity, untuk peta haba), Telegram (notifikasi kepada Keith) dan WhatsApp (Meta), apabila anda menghantar mesej kepada kami.
  • Pemaju, bank, peguam atau pihak lain dalam urusan pembelian, hanya apabila anda meminta kami meneruskan pertanyaan anda, dan hanya butiran yang perlu.
  • Pihak berkuasa, apabila dikehendaki oleh undang-undang.

Sesetengah penyedia ini menyimpan data di pelayan di luar Malaysia.

6. Tempoh penyimpanan

Kami menyimpan rekod pertanyaan selama kami membantu anda, dan sehingga 2 tahun selepas hubungan terakhir, melainkan undang-undang memerlukan tempoh yang lebih lama atau anda menjadi pelanggan. Selepas itu, rekod akan dipadam.

7. Keselamatan

Rekod pertanyaan disimpan dalam akaun Google peribadi yang hanya boleh diakses oleh Keith, dan laman web ini menggunakan sambungan HTTPS yang selamat. Kami mengambil langkah yang munasabah untuk melindungi data anda.

8. Hak anda

Di bawah PDPA, anda boleh meminta kami untuk:

  • memberitahu data peribadi yang kami simpan tentang anda dan memberi salinannya;
  • membetulkan data yang salah, tidak lengkap atau lapuk;
  • berhenti menggunakan data anda untuk pemasaran, atau mengehadkan penggunaannya;
  • menarik balik persetujuan dan memadam data anda, melainkan kami wajib menyimpannya oleh undang-undang.

Hantar permintaan anda melalui WhatsApp atau e-mel. Kami akan menjawab dalam tempoh 21 hari, dan mungkin meminta anda mengesahkan identiti terlebih dahulu.

9. Hubungi kami
