Are there freehold projects in Selangor?

Yes. Freehold projects here include Amaya @ BDR Sri Damansara, ARRA Residences, Daya Residence, Laverra, Linari Kwasa Damansara, RIA, Westfield Residences.

When will the new projects in Selangor be completed?

Expected completion ranges from 2026 to 2031, depending on the project. Some projects complete a set number of months after the advertising permit (APDL).

Can foreigners buy property in Selangor?

Yes. Foreign buyers in Selangor must meet the state minimum purchase price, currently RM2 million, and need state consent.
