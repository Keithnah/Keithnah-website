Buying in KL City Centre: common questions

How many new launch projects are there in KL City Centre?

Keith currently lists 15 projects in KL City Centre: Ascott Star Residence, Cappella Embassy, Centrix, Clouthaus, Dawn KL City Centre, Divine, Eaton Residence, Hanaz Suites @ KL City Centre, Jewel (Tower 2, Oxley Towers), MABA Suitez, Phoeniz Suites @ KL City Centre, Royal Lexis, So/KL (Tower 1, Oxley Towers), Sutera Suites, The Conlay.

What is the lowest starting price for a new property in KL City Centre?

The lowest published starting price is about RM595,000 (gross) at Sutera Suites. Prices change with each phase and unit; ask Keith for the latest figures.

Are there freehold projects in KL City Centre?

Yes. Freehold projects here include Ascott Star Residence, Clouthaus, Dawn KL City Centre, Hanaz Suites @ KL City Centre, Jewel (Tower 2, Oxley Towers), Phoeniz Suites @ KL City Centre, Royal Lexis, So/KL (Tower 1, Oxley Towers), The Conlay.

When will the new projects in KL City Centre be completed?

Expected completion ranges from 2021 to 2032, depending on the project. Some projects complete a set number of months after the advertising permit (APDL).

Can foreigners buy property in KL City Centre?

Yes. Foreign buyers in Kuala Lumpur must meet the state minimum purchase price, currently RM1 million for most residential property types, and need state consent.
