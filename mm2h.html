Malaysia My Second Home — a long-stay visa programme that lets qualifying foreigners live in Malaysia on a renewable multi-year pass. Here's what it is, and how the three tiers compare.

What Is MM2H

A simple explanation

Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) is a government-run visa programme that allows foreign nationals and their dependents to reside in Malaysia on a long-term, renewable pass — without needing a local employer or business.

Approved applicants place a fixed deposit with a Malaysian bank and typically also purchase property above a set minimum value. In return, they receive a multi-year visa that can be renewed, and can bring a spouse and dependents under the same application.

The programme was restructured in 2024 into three tiers — Silver, Gold and Platinum — each with its own financial requirements and visa duration. The higher the tier, the larger the deposit and property purchase, and the longer the visa validity.

MM2H is separate from property ownership itself — it doesn't require buying real estate to qualify on its own, but combining the two is common among Keith's clients who want both a home and a long-stay pass in Kuala Lumpur.

Fixed Deposit

Placed with an approved Malaysian bank; partly withdrawable after the first year.

Property Purchase

A minimum-value real estate purchase in Malaysia, in addition to the deposit.

Multi-Year Visa

A renewable long-stay pass — 5, 15 or 20 years depending on tier.

Family Inclusion

Spouse and eligible dependents can typically be included under one application.
