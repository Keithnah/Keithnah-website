Overview

About Royal Suites Pavilion Damansara Heights.

Royal Suites is a 51-storey freehold residential tower of 490 units within the 16-acre Pavilion Damansara Heights masterplan, directly linked to the Pavilion Damansara Heights MRT station and a lifestyle mall of over 1 million sq. ft. Layouts range from 452 sqft suites to 1,679 sqft dual-key residences, with a courtyard and pool deck on Level 6 and a heated sky pool, sky lounge and spa facilities on Level 51. Residents also enjoy Pavilion Concierge Services.
