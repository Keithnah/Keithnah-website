About Amara Residences

Amara Residences is a freehold 28-storey mixed development in Ara Damansara with 412 serviced apartments on 2.36 acres. Homes run from 630 to 1,230 sqft, starting at Level 10, with facilities on Level 9 and a sky pool on Level 29.
