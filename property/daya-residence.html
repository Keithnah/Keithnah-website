About Daya Residence

Daya Residence is a low-density freehold development of 260 homes on 6.52 acres in Kwasa Damansara, opposite the 42-acre Taman Bandar Kwasa Damansara park. Phase 2 offers 218 condominium units from 947 to 1,572 sqft and 7 three-storey town villas of 3,810 sqft, from RM631,800 (gross, around RM680 psf), while Phase 1 comprises 34 semi-detached homes and 1 bungalow from 3,329 to 5,004 sqft, with a standalone clubhouse and swimming pool.

Location

Daya Residence sits in Kwasa Damansara, a 2,259-acre township with about 250 acres of green space and nine public parks, close to Kwasa Damansara and Kwasa Sentral MRT stations, with the NKVE, DASH and LDP highways and Kota Damansara, Sunway Giza and 1 Utama within reach.
