About Keeperz Suites

Keeperz Suites is a freehold serviced-apartment development of 493 units in two towers on 2.14 acres in Bandar George Town, Penang, with units facing Penang Bridge and the mainland or Bukit Dumbar. Choose from 484 sqft studio layouts or 581 sqft dual-key layouts, from RM820,100 (gross, from about RM1,646 psf), with sky facilities on Level 21 including a sky pool, sky gym and co-working space.
