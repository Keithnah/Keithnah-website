About The Atera

The Atera is Paramount Property's transit-oriented twin-tower development in Section 14, Petaling Jaya, just 400m from Asia Jaya LRT station via a covered walkway. Phase 2 (Tower 2) rises 52 storeys with 788 residential units and 5 retail lots, offering semi-furnished serviced apartments from 775 to 1,420 sqft, 61 lifestyle facilities across a Level 7 podium and Level 48 & 52 sky lounges, and Smart Home features.

Location

The Atera sits in Section 14, Petaling Jaya, a 400m covered walk from Asia Jaya LRT station on the Kelana Jaya Line. It links to the Federal Highway (2.0 km), LDP (5.2 km), SPRINT (6.0 km), NPE (7.0 km) and NKVE (8.0 km), and is close to universities, hospitals and lifestyle destinations including University of Malaya, UM Specialist Centre, Amcorp Mall and Gaswinn, Section 17 and Bangsar.

  • Asia Jaya LRT Station (400m)
  • Federal Highway (2.0 km)
  • SPRINT Expressway (6.0 km)
  • Damansara–Puchong Expressway (5.2 km)
  • New Pantai Expressway (7.0 km)
  • NKVE (8.0 km)
