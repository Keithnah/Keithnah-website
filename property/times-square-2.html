About Times Square 2

Times Square 2 is a freehold, 41-storey mixed development by Berjaya Times Square Sdn Bhd, built right beside Imbi Monorail Station in the heart of Bukit Bintang. The single-block tower holds 629 residential units and 17 commercial units (including a sky restaurant) above 6 levels of carpark, with GreenRE Gold certification and a pet-friendly policy. Suites range from 488 sqft 1-bedroom units to 1,356 sqft 3-bedroom layouts, with a dedicated 7th-floor Urban Deck and 41st-floor Sky Deck for recreation and a 40-metre infinity pool framed by the KL skyline.

Location

Located on Jalan Imbi in Bukit Bintang, directly beside Imbi Monorail Station, with Bukit Bintang MRT, Hang Tuah LRT, Pavilion KL, LaLaport BBCC, Merdeka 118, TRX and KL City Centre all within a 2.2km radius — placing residents at the centre of KL's shopping, dining and nightlife district.
