Overview

About Zenia @ ParkCity Damansara.

Zenia is a 55-acre enclave within ParkCity Damansara, Petaling Jaya, set beside a hill and pond view. It combines 442 three-storey ParkHomes with 643 Condovillas across five blocks (9 to 23 storeys) — a low-density 20 units per acre, with a resort-style facilities deck of pools, a reading and work loft, multipurpose hall and kindergarten.

Before you look

Worth knowing first.

  1. 01

    Low-density 55-acre enclave: 1,085 homes at 20 units per acre, with hill and pond views

  2. 02

    442 three-storey ParkHomes (3,150–3,980 sqft, 4 to 6 bedrooms) with a family lounge and car porch

  3. 03

    643 Condovillas across five blocks, 3 to 5 bedrooms (1,691–4,247 sqft), each with a private lobby; select types with private garden

  4. 04

    Facilities deck: main pool, family pool, kid's pool, reading & work loft, BBQ pavilion, multipurpose hall, multi court, outdoor fitness and kindergarten

  5. 05

    Condovillas include air-conditioning, water heater, kitchen cabinets, hood and hob and engineered timber flooring in rooms
