About Mezon @ Park Enclave

Mezon @ Park Enclave is a freehold serviced-apartment development of two 46-level towers with 602 units on about 2.75 acres at Batu Kawan, Penang, fronting a 25-acre Central Park. Japanese-inspired in design, it offers single-key, dual-key and tri-key layouts from 862 to 1,410 sqft, with 2 car parks per unit and facilities that include a 50 m spring pool, onsen, glass gym and pickleball court on Level 8. Pricing starts from RM595,000 (gross), with completion targeted for 2030.

Location

Mezon @ Park Enclave fronts the 25-acre Central Park at Batu Kawan, with Columbia Asia Hospital Batu Kawan about 800 m away, IKEA Batu Kawan and Klippa Shopping Centre about 1.1 km, Design Village Outlet Mall 1.5 km and Batu Kawan Industrial Park 3.5 km, and close access to the Penang Second Bridge and North-South Expressway.
