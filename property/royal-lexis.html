Overview

About Royal Lexis.

Royal Lexis is a freehold, Lexis Hotel Group-managed serviced residence on Jalan Sultan Ismail, where every single unit comes with its own private pool. Fully furnished and hotel-managed, it is built for both hands-off investors and professionals who want to live in the heart of the KL City Centre corridor.
