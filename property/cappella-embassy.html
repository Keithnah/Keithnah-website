About Cappella Embassy

Cappella Embassy is TSLAW Land's reimagining of the former D'Rapport, set on 9.12 acres along Jalan Ampang in Kuala Lumpur's embassy corridor. Five towers of up to 38 storeys hold 1,099 condominium units, plus 43 retail shops and offices, with layouts from 1,109 sqft 2-bedroom homes to 10,635 sqft. Priced from RM1,577,000 (gross, about RM970 – 1,050 psf), it offers over 50 indoor and outdoor facilities across 280,000 sqft, including a sky bar and observatory deck.

Location

Cappella Embassy sits on Jalan Ampang within Kuala Lumpur's embassy row, near the embassies of Thailand, the USA, China, Indonesia and South Korea. KL City Centre, Avenue K, Ampang Park MRT, Gleneagles Hospital, Prince Court Medical Centre and several international schools are close by, with TRX and Bukit Bintang a short drive away.
