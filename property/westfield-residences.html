About Westfield Residences

Westfield Residences is a freehold development of 928 units in two 44-storey towers on 2.83 acres in USJ 1, Subang Jaya, with 13 units per floor served by 4 + 1 lifts. Homes range from 768 sqft 3-bedroom units to 1,008 sqft 4-bedroom residences, from RM639,000 (gross, from about RM800 psf), with a facility floor featuring a 50m swimming pool, feast garden BBQ deck, co-working lounge, gymnasium, pickleball court, communal kitchen and mini theatre.
