About Ascott Star Residence

Ascott Star Residence is a completed freehold residential address on Levels 35 to 56 of a 58-storey tower in the heart of KL City Centre, Kuala Lumpur, part of a three-tower development of 1,510 units on 4 acres with a 6-storey signature retail boulevard. Layouts range from 700 sqft 1-bedroom homes to 2,089 sqft 3+1 bedroom residences, with a separate lift, 5 passenger lifts plus 1 bomba lift, and residential facilities including a swimming pool, gym and sky park.
