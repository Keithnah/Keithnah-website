About Orion Residence

Orion Residence is a freehold, 46-storey branded-residence tower off Jalan Bukit Bintang, developed by Welton Group. Designed around a double-volume cantilevered sky deck with an infinity pool, sky gym, haute-dining restaurants and a rooftop sky bar, it is positioned for both owner-occupiers and investors drawn to its hotel-style leaseback programme and panoramic KL City Centre/Petronas Towers views.

Location

Situated off Jalan Bukit Bintang and Jalan Gading, in the heart of KL's premier shopping and nightlife district — moments from Pavilion KL, Lot 10 and the Bukit Bintang MRT/Monorail interchange, with the Petronas Twin Towers and KL City Centre skyline forming the backdrop.

  • Bukit Bintang shopping and entertainment precinct

  • Nearby MRT / Monorail connectivity
  • Unblocked views of the KL City Centre skyline and Petronas Twin Towers
