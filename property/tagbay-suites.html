About Tagbay Suites

Tagbay Suites is a coastal-themed, GreenRE-certified suites development in Melaka City — two towers (28 and 29 storeys) on 2.66 acres with 648 units, from 495 sqft studio-style suites to 990 sqft 2+1 bedroom Executive Suites. A Level 6 podium deck brings together a lap pool, water-play zones, a jacuzzi, gymnasium and a garden walking path, with sea views from selected units.

Location

Tagbay Suites is located in Melaka City, a UNESCO World Heritage city and one of Malaysia's leading domestic tourism destinations. Selected units face the Melaka sea and infinity pool, while others look over the city, with the town centre and Pusat Niaga Nakhoda close by.
