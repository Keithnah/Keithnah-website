About The Queenswoodz

The Queenswoodz is EXSIM's newest address in Bukit Jalil City — a 1,004-unit, two-tower residence built around the idea of "the feminine sensuality to a masculine legacy": flowing curves balanced against strong vertical lines, set on 3.44 acres just steps from Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort and the LRT Awan Besar station.

Location

Set in the heart of Bukit Jalil City on a 3.44-acre site, The Queenswoodz sits within walking distance of the LRT Awan Besar station and Novel Park, with Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort, Pavilion Bukit Jalil and the National Stadium all close by.

  • LRT Awan Besar Station (900m)
  • Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort
  • Pavilion Bukit Jalil
  • Bukit Jalil Recreational Park
  • National Stadium Bukit Jalil & Stadium Putra

Who Should Consider The Queenswoodz?

Young Professionals & Couples

Suited to buyers drawn to the compact, efficient Type A1/A2 (807 sqft, 2+1 bedrooms) as a low-maintenance first home close to the LRT.

Growing Families

Suited to buyers looking at Type B, C or D layouts (936–1,410 sqft, up to 4 bedrooms) for more room to grow, with resort-style facilities for family downtime.

Multigenerational & Rental Investors

Suited to buyers interested in the Type D2 dual-key layout for multi-family living, home-office separation, or splitting rental income.
