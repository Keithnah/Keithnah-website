About Laurel Residence

Laurel Residence is a 1,260-unit suite apartment development in Bangsar South, the transit-oriented city next to Kerinchi LRT station. Two towers on 2.1 acres offer partly furnished suites from 495 to 903 sqft, including dual-key Type D1, with a Zen garden, garden pool and a co-working lounge, and covered walkways to the LRT, offices, malls and the award-winning 6-acre park.

Location

Laurel Residence is on Jalan Kerinchi in Bangsar South, connected by a covered link bridge and walkways to Kerinchi LRT station, offices, Nexus, The Sphere and the 6-acre park. Shuttle buses run within Bangsar South, Angkasapuri and Pantai Dalam KTM stations are nearby, and the New Pantai Expressway links to Mid Valley, KL Sentral and Petaling Jaya.
