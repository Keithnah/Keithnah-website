About Imperial Residence Pavilion Damansara Heights

Imperial Residence is a 50-storey freehold luxury residential tower of only 154 units within the 16-acre Pavilion Damansara Heights masterplan. Typical residences of 3,380 and 4,090 sqft offer three to four ensuites with a lanai, with penthouses of 7,459 sqft above. Residents enjoy a Level 6 courtyard with a leisure pool and function spaces, and The Sky at Level 50 with a heated lap pool, sky lounge and spa facilities. Pricing and completion are being finalised — message Keith directly for the latest information.

Location

Imperial Residence sits in Damansara Heights, beside the Pavilion Damansara Heights MRT station (2 stations to KL Sentral and 5 stations to Kuala Lumpur City Centre) and a lifestyle mall of over 1 million sq. ft., close to Kuala Lumpur Golf & Country Club and Bukit Kiara Equestrian & Country Resort.
