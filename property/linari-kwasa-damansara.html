About Linari Kwasa Damansara

Linari is a freehold development of sky villas and park homes on 8.79 acres next to Kwasa Damansara City Centre, 35 steps from Kwasa Damansara MRT station through a private gateway. Two 25-storey towers of 480 condominium homes from 1,158 to 1,637 sqft and 16 Parkhomes rise above a pedestrian-only park with 80% green coverage.

Location

Linari sits on Persiaran Atmosfera beside Kwasa Damansara City Centre, a planned 2,300-acre transit city with 250 acres of parks and 2 MRT stations. A private gateway leads to Kwasa Damansara MRT station 35 steps away, and Subang SkyPark is about 4 km away, with direct links to the major highways.
