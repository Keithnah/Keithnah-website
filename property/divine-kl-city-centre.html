Overview

About Divine.

Divine is an 83-storey, 1,033-unit landmark rising just 400m from the Petronas Twin Towers, linked to the Jalan Ampang–KL City Centre junction via a covered walkway. Developed by Chin Hin Property on 1.88 acres, the tower pairs interiors by Ministry of Design with landscaping by SD2 across four sky-high facility decks, crowned by Malaysia's first and tallest 360° rooftop bar.
