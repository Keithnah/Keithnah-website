About Senna Heights

Senna Heights is a leasehold, low-density development of 780 residential units in two 43-storey towers on 6.5 acres in Damansara Damai, with 18 commercial shop-lots downstairs and forest reserve views. Layouts run from 700 sqft 1-bedroom units to 1,464 sqft 3-bedroom plus study homes, from RM672,800 (gross, from about RM658 psf), with over 60 facilities across the ground floor, Level 4, Level 5 and rooftop.

Location

Senna Heights is in the established township of Damansara Damai, in the northern corridor of Petaling Jaya, with access to the LDP, NSE, NKVE and DUKE highways and the MRT and KTM lines towards Kuala Lumpur, Sungai Buloh and beyond.

  • Damansara Damai MRT station
  • Sri Damansara Barat MRT
  • Sungai Buloh
  • Kota Damansara
  • Bukit Lagong Forest Reserve
  • FRIM Kepong
  • iPlay Park
