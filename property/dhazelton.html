About D'Hazelton

D'Hazelton is a freehold residential development in Farlim, Paya Terubong, Penang, set in the hills at the heart of Penang Island. Phase 1 comprises two towers of 35 and 34 storeys with 1,670 residential units, offered as 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom layouts of about 900 to 1,000 sqft with approximately 10.5 ft ceilings. Facilities total 67 types, including a 170 ft infinity pool, jacuzzi, gym, mini theatre, multipurpose hall, sky lawn and putting green. Pricing starts from RM450,000 (gross), with completion expected in 2029.

Location

D'Hazelton sits in the Farlim neighbourhood of Paya Terubong at the heart of Penang Island, about 2.8 km from Sunshine Mall, 3 km from All Season Place, 8.4 km from Gurney Plaza and Gurney Paragon and 9.3 km from Universiti Sains Malaysia, with Pasar Air Itam, SJK (C) Sin Kang and SMJK Chung Ling also close by.
