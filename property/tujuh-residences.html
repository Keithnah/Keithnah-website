About Tujuh Residences

Tujuh Residences is a leasehold residential development of 573 units in a single 28-storey block of two wings on 2.005 acres in Kwasa Damansara, Selangor. Homes range from 550 sqft 1-bedroom units to 909 sqft 2-bedroom plus studio layouts, from RM501,000 (gross, from about RM865 psf), with 13 to 24 units per floor served by 6 + 1 lifts and a Level 10 podium featuring a swimming pool, gym, yoga deck and BBQ area.
