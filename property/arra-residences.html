About ARRA Residences

ARRA Residences & Retail Park is a freehold integrated transit-oriented development of 1,275 units in three towers on 4.14 acres in Ara Damansara, Petaling Jaya, anchored by a retail park. Tower A is now selling with 721 sqft 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom homes from RM554,000 (gross, from about RM769 psf), while Towers B and C extend the range up to 1,285 sqft.
