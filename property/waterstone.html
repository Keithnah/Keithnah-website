About Waterstone

Waterstone is a freehold residential development on approximately 2.4 acres in Tanjung Bungah, Penang, with two towers (A1 and A2) rising 39 levels and 365 units in total. Spacious 3 and 4-bedroom layouts from 1,334 to 2,195 sqft look out to the sea or the hills, with an approximate ceiling height of 10.5 feet. Expected completion is Q4 2028.
