Overview

About Ren Residences.

Ren Residences is Gaya Kuasa's twin-tower address at Residensi Renaisans Bukit Jalil, designed by GDP Architects in a distinctive 人 ("Ren", meaning people) shape that brings natural sunlight, shorter lift-lobby corridors and scenic Bukit Jalil views. 1,260 units across Tower A and Tower B sit on 5.6 acres with over an acre of family-friendly facilities, close to Tzu Chi International School, LRT stations and five major highways.

Before you look

Worth knowing first.

  1. 01

    Twin 人-shaped towers by GDP Architects — 1,260 units on 5.6 acres, 16 units per floor, 5+1 lifts

  2. 02

    3 to 4+1-bedroom homes from 920–1,680 sqft, from RM537,000 (gross); 2 car parks per unit (3 for Type D)

  3. 03

    Over an acre of facilities: swimming pool, kid's pool, jacuzzi, playground, tennis/futsal and basketball courts, gym, yoga room, co-working space, games room

  4. 04

    3-tier security with face recognition and licence-plate recognition access

  5. 05

    Near Tzu Chi International School, Pavilion Bukit Jalil, IMU and Alam Sutera LRT
