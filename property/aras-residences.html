Overview

About Aras Residences.

Aras Residences is a freehold parkfront development in WCity OUG, off Jalan Awan Besar in south Kuala Lumpur. Two 56-storey towers hold 1,272 homes of 850 and 1,062 sqft, with 2- and 3-bedroom layouts, set beside the OUG township and minutes from Mid Valley, Bukit Jalil and Sri Petaling.

Before you look

Worth knowing first.

  1. 01

    Freehold title in an established south KL neighbourhood

  2. 02

    Two practical layouts: 2 bedrooms (850 sqft) or 3 bedrooms (1,062 sqft), both with 2 bathrooms

  3. 03

    Air-conditioners in the living room and bedrooms, water heaters with a rain shower in the master bath, shower screens and a digital lockset

  4. 04

    Five passenger lifts plus a fire lift per floor, and multi-tier security

  5. 05

    Close to Mid Valley Megamall, Pavilion Bukit Jalil, IMU and Columbia Asia Hospital Bukit Jalil
