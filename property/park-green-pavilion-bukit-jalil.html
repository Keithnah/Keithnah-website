About Park Green Pavilion Bukit Jalil

Park Green is the final residential tower of Bukit Jalil City, the freehold 50-acre integrated township by Malton. Two wings of 47 floors hold 453 freehold family homes from 1,201 to 1,905 sqft, with panoramic views over the 80-acre Bukit Jalil Recreational Park, a dedicated pedestrian bridge to Pavilion Bukit Jalil and concierge services for residents.

Location

Park Green sits within Bukit Jalil City, right next to Pavilion Bukit Jalil and facing the 80-acre Bukit Jalil Recreational Park. Bukit Jalil Highway, the KL-Seremban Expressway and the MEX link it to KL city centre, Cyberjaya, Putrajaya and KLIA, and IMU, Columbia Asia Hospital, Tzu Chi International School and APU are a short drive away.

  • Pavilion Bukit Jalil
  • Bukit Jalil Recreational Park
  • Bukit Jalil National Stadium
  • Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort
  • IMU Healthcare (3.2 km)
  • Columbia Asia Hospital (3.3 km)
