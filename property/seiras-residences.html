About Seiras Residences

Seiras Residences is a freehold serviced-apartment tower of 411 units rising 43 levels on 2.27 acres in Bandar Cassia, Batu Kawan, Penang. Every home is a 1,033 sqft, 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom layout, offered as a dual-key Type A from RM578,000 (gross) or a triple-key Type B from RM641,000 (gross). Facilities include a 50 m x 25 m Olympic-size pool, pickleball court, gymnasium, bouldering wall, co-working space and co-kitchen. Completion is tentatively 2028 Q1.
