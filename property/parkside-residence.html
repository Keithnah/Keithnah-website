About Parkside Residence

Parkside Residence is a 62-storey leasehold tower of 693 units on 1.18 acres in Kuala Lumpur, overlooking a central park. Homes range from 485 sqft 1-bedroom flexi units to 1,325 sqft 3-bedroom residences, from RM665,000 (gross), with 14 units per floor served by 7+1 lifts and multi-tier security with facial recognition at the lift lobbies. Units are partially furnished and the development targets GreenRE Gold (provisional).
