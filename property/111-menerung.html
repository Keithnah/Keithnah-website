About 111 Menerung

111 Menerung is a low-density freehold development of 111 residences in 23 storeys on 0.83 acres in Damansara Heights, Kuala Lumpur, with only 6 to 10 units per floor and 4 lifts. Homes range from 1,001 sqft to 3,714 sqft, 2 to 3+1 bedrooms, from RM1,745,000 (gross, from about RM1,680 psf), with multi-tier security and 1 to 3 car parks per unit.
