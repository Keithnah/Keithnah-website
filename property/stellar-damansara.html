About Stellar Damansara

Stellar Damansara by OCR is a low-density luxury residence of just 88 homes in two blocks off the Sprint Highway in Damansara, Petaling Jaya. Homes from 2,028 to 2,530 sqft with 3+1 to 5 bedrooms are served by private lifts, with only 2 units per floor in Tower A and 4 in Tower B.
