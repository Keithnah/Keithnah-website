About Andabreeze Residences

Andabreeze Residences is a freehold serviced-residence development of 1,406 units in two towers (53 and 46 floors) on 4.31 acres in Bandar Cassia, Batu Kawan, Penang, with sea views towards the Penang Second Bridge. Layouts run from 636 sqft 2-bedroom homes to 1,280 sqft 4-bedroom residences with 10 ft ceilings, a Level 9 podium of over 50 facilities and a rooftop sky deck. Tower A is released first, with completion expected in Q2 2030.
