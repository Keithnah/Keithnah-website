About G'Vinton

G'Vinton is a freehold serviced-apartment tower of 508 units on 1.14 acres along Jalan Sultan Ahmad Shah, Penang's Millionaires' Row in George Town, rising 39 floors. Layouts include compact 387 sqft and 474 sqft units and a 1,356 sqft duplex, with sea-view and city-view options, a 3.15 m ceiling height and a Level 39 infinity pool. Expected completion is Q3 2028.
