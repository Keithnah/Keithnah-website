About MABA Suitez

MABA Suitez is a two-tower service apartment and office suite development in the heart of Kuala Lumpur's city centre, directly beside Menara Merdeka Maybank. Tower A (54 storeys) and Tower B (50 storeys) hold 914 units on 1.328 acres, from 323 sqft office-suite studios to 657 sqft dual-key suites, with Merdeka MRT station about 160m (a 2-minute walk) away.

Location

MABA Suitez sits on Jalan Hang Jebat in KL City Centre, about 160m from Merdeka MRT station on the Kajang line. From there, Pavilion KL is one station away, TRX two stations and KL Sentral two stations, with Merdeka 118, Stadium Negara and the KL City Centre and KL Tower skyline all within the surrounding city centre.

  • Merdeka MRT Station (KG17, about 160m)
  • Merdeka 118 & Menara Merdeka Maybank
  • Pavilion Kuala Lumpur (1 MRT station)
  • Tun Razak Exchange (2 MRT stations)
  • Pasar Seni & Central Market (1 MRT station)
  • KL Sentral via Muzium Negara (2 MRT stations)
  • Stadium Negara & Chin Woo Stadium
