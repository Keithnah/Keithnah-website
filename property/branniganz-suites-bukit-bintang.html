About Branniganz Suites @ Bukit Bintang

Branniganz Suites @ Bukit Bintang is a 759-unit, single-tower development by EXSIM rising directly beside Berjaya Times Square in the heart of Kuala Lumpur's Bukit Bintang entertainment district. Its name draws on the Irish word "Branigan" — meaning full of energy and cheer — and the 51-level tower (marketed as a 50-storey property) splits Wing A Office Suites from Wing B Serviced Suites across a 0.56-acre site, with units ranging from a 344 sqft Studio Office to a 678 sqft 1-Bedroom Serviced Suite. EXSIM backs the launch with its Mana Mana Hospitality rental programme, offering Non-HDA buyers a 6% p.a. minimum rental return (or 80/20 profit sharing, whichever is higher) or, for the first 100 units, an 8% p.a. guaranteed rental return for 3 years, with HDA units earning a 4% p.a. minimum rental return or 80/20 profit sharing. All units come fully furnished by EXSIM.

Location

Sited in the heart of Bukit Bintang, immediately beside Sungai Wang Plaza and the AirAsia–Bukit Bintang Monorail Station, within the block bounded by Jalan Bukit Bintang and Jalan Sultan Ismail. Berjaya Times Square and Pan Pacific Kuala Lumpur stand directly across the junction, with Imbi Plaza, Merdeka 118 and BBCC to the west, KL Tower and Sungei Wang to the north, TRX and Times Square to the south, and Pavilion KL, Park Royal and the Royal Selangor Golf Club to the east.

  • Adjoining Berjaya Times Square & the Bukit Bintang Monorail Station
  • Near Sungai Wang Plaza, Pavilion KL & Lot 10
  • On the Jalan Bukit Bintang / Jalan Sultan Ismail corridor
  • Short reach to TRX, Merdeka 118, KL City Centre & KL Tower
