About Eaton Residence

Eaton Residences is a completed, 51-storey freehold-style (99-year leasehold) luxury serviced residence by City View Ventures Sdn Bhd (GSH Corporation / Naza), set on a 1.42-acre site on Jalan Kia Peng, within walking distance of the Petronas Twin Towers and Royal Selangor Golf Club. The single tower holds 632 units from 635 sqft 1-bedroom suites to 2,831 sqft 4-bedroom penthouses, anchored by a five-level facilities podium — Grand Oasis lobby, Adventure Meadow garden, Tranquil Plateau sky lounge, Sky Gym and the rooftop Sky Deck with its infinity pool — all positioned to capture KL City Centre and Royal Selangor Golf Club views.
