About Laverra

Laverra is a freehold residential development of 1,008 units in two towers (55 and 60 storeys) on 2.83 acres in Bandar Sri Damansara, designed for multi-generation living with up to four generations in mind. Homes range from 725 sqft to 1,150 sqft, 2 to 4 bedrooms, from RM696,700 (gross), with a Level 9 facility podium featuring an infinity lap pool, jacuzzi pod, playground, indoor gymnasium and elderly-friendly areas.

Location

Laverra sits in Bandar Sri Damansara, with Sri Damansara West MRT Station on the MRT Putrajaya Line about 500m away, plus the Sri Damansara Sentral and East stations, Kepong Sentral KTM Commuter Station and neighbourhood retail, schools and medical centres nearby.
