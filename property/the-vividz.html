About The Vividz

The Vividz @ Bukit Jalil is EXSIM's new twin-tower residence inside KL Wellness City — Malaysia's first fully integrated healthcare & wellness township. Built around the "Colores" concept of vibrant, colour-therapy-led living, the 1,138-unit development (plus 7 retail units) sits on 2.55 acres right beside the upcoming KL International Hospital, with a shuttle bus service linking residents straight to Pavilion Bukit Jalil and Awan Besar LRT.

Location

The Vividz sits on Jalan Impian Indah within the KL Wellness City master plan in Bukit Jalil, directly beside the future KL International Hospital and The Nobel Healthcare Park. A proposed shuttle bus links residents to Pavilion Bukit Jalil, The Link Bukit Jalil and Awan Besar LRT station, with KESAS, NPE, Maju, Sprint, ELITE and Sungai Besi expressways all within easy reach.

  • KL Wellness City & KL International Hospital (on-site)
  • Pavilion Bukit Jalil (1.4km)
  • Awan Besar LRT Station (Sri Petaling Line)
  • Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort
  • Bukit Jalil Recreational Park
