About Brixton Causeway Square

Brixton is a freehold serviced-apartment development of 1,257 units in two 60-storey towers (Tower B1 with 537 units and Tower B2 with 720 units) within the 7.75-acre Causewayz Square masterplan in Johor Bahru. Layouts run from 474 sqft one-bedroom homes to 850 sqft three-bedroom and dual-key residences, priced from RM691,000 (gross). Level 12 podium facilities include the Ocean Pool, infinity pool, hoops court, multipurpose hall and a Sprint Track, with sky gardens and BBQ on Level 57. Completion is 60 months after APDL.
