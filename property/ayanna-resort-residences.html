Overview

About Ayanna Resort Residences.

Ayanna Resort Residences is a freehold, resort-style residence with a residential title in Bukit Jalil. Two towers of 42 and 44 storeys on 4.9 acres hold 824 large family homes from 1,155 to 2,453 sqft, with 2 to 4 car park bays each, a 1.3-acre recreational park with an interactive stream and semi-D style sky living.

Before you look

Worth knowing first.

  1. 01

    Freehold with a residential title, uncommon for high-rise homes in KL

  2. 02

    Large layouts from 1,155 sqft, with 2 to 4 car park bays per unit

  3. 03

    1.3-acre recreational park with an interactive stream, and pet-friendly facilities

  4. 04

    Kitchen cabinet with hood and hob, smart home system, water heater, air-conditioners and shower screens

  5. 05

    EV charging stations and a low maintenance fee of RM0.27 psf

Location

Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur

Ayanna is in the mature Bukit Jalil neighbourhood, linked by the Bukit Jalil Highway, KL-Seremban Expressway, LDP, KESAS, MEX and NPE. LRT Alam Sutera is 1.5 km and LRT Kinrara BK5 2.5 km away, Pavilion Bukit Jalil 3.5 km and Bukit Jalil Recreational Park 4 km, with Tzu Chi International School, IMU and Sunway University within about 7 km.
