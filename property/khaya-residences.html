About Khaya Residences

Khaya Residences is a 61-storey leasehold serviced residence of 795 units on 3.88 acres in Bangsar, Kuala Lumpur, with 15,000 sqft of retail space. Homes range from 630 sqft 1-bedroom units to 1,321 sqft 3-bedroom residences, from RM893,000 (gross, from about RM1,157 psf), and come partially furnished. Facilities spread over 40 recreational features include a 50m infinity lap pool at Level 8, a rooftop gym at Level 60 and a Sky Lawn at Level 61.
