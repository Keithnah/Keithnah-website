About ForestHill Residences

ForestHill Residences is a leasehold residential development of 618 units in two towers (38 and 35 storeys) on 4.39 acres in Damansara Perdana, set beside a calm lake, forest and rolling hills. Homes range from 953 sqft 3-bedroom units to 1,527 sqft 4-bedroom dual-key layouts, from RM643,300 (gross, from about RM675 psf), with 10 units per floor, 3 + 1 lifts and 38 lifestyle facilities across the podium and gardens.
