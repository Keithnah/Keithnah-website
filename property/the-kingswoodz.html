About The Kingswoodz

The Kingswoodz @ Bukit Jalil is EXSIM's 3-tower, 1,558-unit flagship in Bukit Jalil, its design inspired by the intricate inlays of kingwood furniture. Rising up to 46 storeys on a 3.45-acre site, it's a 900m walk to Awan Besar LRT station and minutes from Pavilion Bukit Jalil, with resort-style facilities spread across Level G, the Level 8 "Bordering Infinity" deck and Level 46 sky terraces.

Location

The Kingswoodz sits directly off Lebuhraya Shah Alam in Bukit Jalil, 900m from Awan Besar LRT station and a 3-minute drive to Pavilion Bukit Jalil. It's surrounded by established schools (SJK(C) Lai Meng, Tzu Chi International School, IMU), Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort and Bukit Jalil Recreational Park, with the Shah Alam, Puchong, KL and Seremban expressway links all close by — KLIA is under 45 minutes away.

  • Awan Besar LRT Station (900m)
  • Pavilion Bukit Jalil (3 min drive)
  • Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort
  • Bukit Jalil Recreational Park (1km)
  • SJK(C) Lai Meng (650m)
