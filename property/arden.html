About Arden

Arden is a freehold serviced-apartment tower of 618 units and 4 commercial lots rising 68 storeys (260 m) on 1.735 acres at One Bukit Senyum, Johor Bahru. Layouts run from 797 sqft 2-bedroom homes to 1,700 sqft 4-bedroom residences, supported by leisure and business floors on Levels 8 and 9, a Sky Entertainment Floor on Level 50 and a Sky Garden on Level 68. Completion is targeted for the first half of 2029.
