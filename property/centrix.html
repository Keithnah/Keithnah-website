About Centrix

Centrix The Station, KL City Centre is a single 47-storey transit-oriented mixed development built directly above Kuala Lumpur's Dang Wangi LRT (KJ12) underground station, with retail and commercial space on its lower floors and 857 SOHO units from the 12th to 46th floor.
