About Queens Residences 3

Queens Residences 3 is a freehold waterfront residential tower of 532 units on 2.9 acres of reclaimed land in Bayan Lepas, Penang, rising 25 floors with 28 units per floor. Layouts run from 950 sqft dual-key homes to 1,400 sqft 3-bedroom residences, with park-view and sea-view units and a 10 ft ceiling height. Expected completion is the end of 2027.
