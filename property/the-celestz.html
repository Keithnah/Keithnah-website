About The Celestz

The Celestz @ Kebun Teh is a leasehold serviced-apartment tower of 735 units rising 44 storeys on 1.8 acres in Kebun Teh, Johor Bahru, with a celestial-inspired podium of pools, a half basketball court, gymnasium and co-working space, and Starlight BBQ and pavilion on Level 42. Layouts run from 581 sqft 1-bedroom-plus-study homes to 915 sqft 3-bedroom residences, priced from RM543,800 (gross). Completion is 52 months after APDL.
