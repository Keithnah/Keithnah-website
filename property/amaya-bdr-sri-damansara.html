About Amaya @ BDR Sri Damansara

Amaya is a freehold, resort-inspired high-rise in Bandar Sri Damansara, themed around Zen-inspired Japanese living. Tower A rises 68 storeys with 634 units, from 1-bedroom suites to 3-bedroom homes, a direct link to the MRT, connection to a shopping mall and an extensive sky facilities deck on Levels 67 and 68.

Location

Amaya sits in Bandar Sri Damansara, with Sri Damansara Sentral MRT station about 0.5 km away and Kepong Sentral KTM about 2.0 km. ParkCity Medical Centre and The International School @ ParkCity are about 2.5 km away, The Curve, IKEA and IPC about 4.5 km, Mont Kiara and Desa Sri Hartamas about 5.0 km and Pavilion Damansara Heights about 8.0 km.
