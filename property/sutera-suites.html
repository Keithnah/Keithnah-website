About Sutera Suites

SUTERA | KL City Gateway is a transit-oriented residential and serviced-apartment development in Kampung Baru, linked to KL City Centre via the Saloma Pedestrian Bridge, within a large integrated mixed-use masterplan of residences, offices and retail.
