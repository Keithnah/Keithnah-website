About The Aldenz

The Aldenz is EXSIM's single-tower, 662-unit address in Damansara Perdana, Petaling Jaya, themed around "Colonial Class in the Hills" with a colonial-style retail street at its doorstep. Rising 40 levels on 2.555 acres, it sits beside Bandar Utama MRT/LRT 3 interchange, 1 Utama and Damansara Specialist Centre, with a podium pool deck, yoga alcove, herb garden and pet-friendly park.

Location

The Aldenz is set in Damansara Perdana at the crossroads of the Lebuhraya Damansara–Puchong (LDP), NKVE and Sprint Highway. Bandar Utama MRT/LRT 3 interchange, 1 Utama Shopping Centre and Damansara Specialist Centre are close by, with The Curve, IPC, Mutiara Damansara, Tropicana and Damansara Heights all within easy reach.
