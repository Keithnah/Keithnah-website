About Accent Residence

Accent is a low-density serviced apartment tower in the heart of Petaling Jaya, on Jalan Kemajuan in Section 13. With only 8 units per floor served by private lift lobbies, it offers landed-width living and dining (20 to 24 ft), flexible layouts and lanai-inspired outdoor spaces, plus selected garden units — homes designed to adapt to the owner's life stage.

Location

Accent is located on Jalan Kemajuan in Section 13, Petaling Jaya, positioned along the main road and Jalan 13/1 beside Plaza 33. Jaya Shopping Centre, Jaya One and Jaya 33 are close by, with Mid Valley, 1 Utama and Paradigm within a short drive and the Asia Jaya LRT and Phileo Damansara MRT stations nearby.
