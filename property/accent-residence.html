Overview

About Accent Residence.

Accent is a low-density serviced apartment tower in the heart of Petaling Jaya, on Jalan Kemajuan in Section 13. With only 8 units per floor served by private lift lobbies, it offers landed-width living and dining (20 to 24 ft), flexible layouts and lanai-inspired outdoor spaces, plus selected garden units — homes designed to adapt to the owner's life stage.

Before you look

Worth knowing first.

  1. 01

    Low-density tower: 231 serviced apartments over 8 units per floor, each with a private lift lobby (8 private lifts)

  2. 02

    Landed-sized living and dining width of 20, 22 and 24 ft with side-by-side layout

  3. 03

    Semi-D suites in the sky and garden units; lanai and balcony options on selected types

  4. 04

    Facilities floor of about 4,600 sqm; 2.4 m main doors and 2.4 m sliding doors at the living hall

  5. 05

    Air-conditioning provided in each unit; Roca sanitary ware; age-friendly bathroom attached to the junior suite
