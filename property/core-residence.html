About Core Residence

Core Residence is a freehold address in Kuala Lumpur's Golden Triangle, next to the Tun Razak Exchange (TRX) financial district. Three towers on 1.65 acres — Towers 1 & 2 (580 units, 50 levels) and the boutique Tower 3 (120 units, 44 levels, fully furnished) — with a Level 7 podium and rooftop facilities and GreenRE-oriented sustainable design.

Location

Core Residence sits in KL's Golden Triangle at the edge of the Tun Razak Exchange, Malaysia's new international financial district. MRT, major highways and the Bukit Bintang and KL City Centre shopping and business hubs are all close by, making it a central address for professionals and investors.

  • TRX Exchange & Exchange 106
  • Pavilion Kuala Lumpur & Bukit Bintang
  • KL City Centre & Suria KLCC
  • MRT Tun Razak Exchange station
  • Jalan Tun Razak & Jalan Pudu access
  • Hospitals and international schools in the city centre
