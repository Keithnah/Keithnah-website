About Erinaz Suites

Erinaz Suites is a freehold, GreenRE-certified serviced apartment tower in Bandar Baru Kubang Kerian, Kota Bharu, near Hospital USM. One 24-storey building on 1.076 acres holds 361 units: studios (355 sqft), 2-bedroom units (549 sqft) and 2+1-bedroom dual-key units (893 sqft), with an infinity pool and sky facilities on Level 23. Purchase is open to Kelantanese Bumiputera buyers only.

Location

Erinaz Suites is in Seksyen 84, Bandar Baru Kubang Kerian, in the Kota Bharu district of Kelantan. Hospital Universiti Sains Malaysia and its health campus are next to the township, Kota Bharu city centre is a short drive away and the major shopping malls are within about 10 km. The future East Coast Rail Link station is about 21 minutes away.
