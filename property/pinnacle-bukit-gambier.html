About Pinnacle Bukit Gambier

Pinnacle Bukit Gambier is a freehold serviced-apartment development of 982 units in two 45-storey towers on a 7.96-acre site in Gelugor, Penang, with 56% dedicated green space and a 4.48-acre private green lung. Layouts run from 1,077 to 1,734 sqft with 3 to 4 bedrooms, and a Level 14 leisure deck with 40 facilities. Expected completion is Q1 2030.
