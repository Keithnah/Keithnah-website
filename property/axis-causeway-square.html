About Axis Causeway Square

Axis is a freehold serviced-apartment tower of 908 units rising 62 levels, with 20 units per floor, within the 7.75-acre Causewayz Square masterplan in Johor Bahru. Two layouts are offered, a 366 sqft studio and a 592 sqft dual-key 2-bedroom, priced from RM580,300 (gross). Level 60 sky facilities include a Wavescape infinity pool, Seascape wading pool, Sky BBQ deck, jacuzzi lounge and coworking lounge. Completion is 60 months after APDL.
