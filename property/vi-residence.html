About Vi Residence

Vi Residence is a freehold serviced-apartment development of two 29-storey towers with 514 units on about 3.43 acres at Batu Kawan, Penang, with 257 units per tower. Two layouts are offered: a 1,000 sqft 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom Type A from RM550,000 (gross), and a 1,250 sqft dual-key Type B with 3 bedrooms plus a studio from RM660,000 (gross). Facilities include a 48.5 m swimming pool, sky gym, co-working lounge and basketball half court, and every unit comes with 2 car parks. Completion is 4 years from the SPA date.
