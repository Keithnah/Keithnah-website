About D'Evia

D'Evia is EXSIM's single-tower, 440-unit address in Kwasa Damansara, one of five signature plots inspired by the Five Elements of Nature — its design themed around Water. Rising 32 storeys on 2.014 acres, it sits about 600m from Kwasa Sentral MRT station within a transit-oriented township, with an Evia Arcade, Pulse Gym, Flow Lobby and a GreenRE-certified, eco-responsible design.

Location

D'Evia is positioned in Kwasa Damansara, one of the Klang Valley's newest transit-oriented townships, about 600m from Kwasa Sentral MRT station. It connects to Kuala Lumpur and Petaling Jaya via the MRT network and the Guthrie Corridor Expressway, NKVE, DASH and LDP highways, close to HELP University, Sunway Giza, Tropicana Gardens Mall, The Curve, IKEA and 1 Utama.
