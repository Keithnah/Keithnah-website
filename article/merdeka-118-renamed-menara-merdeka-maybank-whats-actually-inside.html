Merdeka 118 Renamed Menara Merdeka Maybank — What's Actually Inside?

25 Sep 2026 · Keith Nah

The naming of Merdeka 118 — one of the tallest towers on Kuala Lumpur's skyline — has shifted again, with the tower reportedly renamed Menara Merdeka Maybank following a corporate naming-rights arrangement. Beyond the name change itself, the more useful question for buyers and tenants nearby is what the tower actually contains, and what a rebrand like this signals for the surrounding district.

Why Naming Rights Matter for a Tower Like This

Corporate naming-rights deals on landmark towers are common in major financial centres worldwide, and they typically signal a long-term anchor-tenant commitment rather than a purely cosmetic rebrand — a bank doesn't put its name on a skyline-defining building without intending to occupy it for the long haul.

For the surrounding property market, a strong, named anchor tenant tends to reinforce the district's positioning as a credible, long-term commercial address. That matters more to nearby investors over time than the name on the building itself.

What's Inside the Tower

In line with towers of this scale, the bulk of the built-up area is typically Grade-A office space, with a retail and dining podium anchoring the base of the building and drawing daytime footfall from the surrounding district.

An observation deck near the top is a common feature of super-tall towers like this one, offering panoramic views of the KL skyline, alongside a hospitality component — hotel floors are typically part of this class of mixed-use development, designed to keep the tower active well beyond standard office hours.

What It Means for Buyers Nearby

A fully-leased, well-anchored landmark tower supports footfall and daytime population for surrounding retail and F&B, which in turn supports rental demand for nearby residential and mixed-use developments in the district.

Buyers evaluating nearby projects should treat the naming and anchor-tenant news as a signal of district confidence, not as a reason alone to pay a premium — the fundamentals of any specific nearby project still need to stand on their own merits.
