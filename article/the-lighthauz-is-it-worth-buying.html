The Lighthauz: Is It Worth Buying? A Plain Guide

14 Nov 2026 · Keith Nah

The Lighthauz is a serviced apartment project in Gelugor, Penang. This guide covers what you get, the location, the layouts and prices, who it suits and what to compare before you decide.

What The Lighthauz is

The Lighthauz is a freehold serviced-apartment tower of 671 units on 2.03 acres in Gelugor, Penang, rising 57 storeys with views towards Bukit Dumbar, Penang Bridge, the mainland and Gelugor. Layouts run from 732 sqft 2-room units to 1,001 sqft 3-room units, from RM864,500 (gross, from about RM1,117 psf), with sky facilities on Levels 56 and 57 including a sky pool, sky lounge and games room.

It is developed by Exsim Development Sdn Bhd. Tenure: Freehold. Completion: 60 months after APDL. Units: 671 units in 1 block; 57 storeys (46 storeys of units). Land area: 2.03 acres.

Location and getting around

The Lighthauz is in Gelugor, Penang, a waterfront address with direct access to key highways towards Penang Bridge, George Town and Penang International Airport, and the upcoming Mutiara LRT line in the surrounding mature neighbourhood.

Places nearby include Penang Bridge, Penang Mainland views, Bukit Dumbar, Gelugor, Mutiara LRT (upcoming).

Layouts and prices

The Lighthauz has 12 layouts, 732 – 1,001 sqft, with 2 – 3 Bedrooms. Prices: From RM864,500 (Gross). Price per sqft: From RM1,117 psf (Gross).

The layouts: Type A1 (732 sqft), Type A1a (732 sqft), Type A2 (732 sqft), Type A2a (732 sqft), Type B1 (1,001 sqft), Type B1a (1,001 sqft), Type B2 (1,001 sqft), Type B2a (1,001 sqft), Type C1 (1,001 sqft), Type C1a (1,001 sqft).

Who it suits

Investors: Freehold units from RM864,500 (gross) in a 671-unit waterfront tower in Gelugor.

Sky-View Seekers: High-floor units facing Bukit Dumbar, Penang Bridge or the mainland, with sky pool and sky lounge on Levels 56 and 57.

Penang Waterfront Buyers: A premier waterfront address with direct highway access and the upcoming Mutiara LRT nearby.

What to compare before you decide

The tenure is Freehold, so compare it with other projects you are looking at. Completion is 60 months after APDL, so match it with when you need the home. The maintenance fee is Est. RM0.46 psf (incl. sinking fund). Car parks: 1,370 car parks (incl. 5 OKU); 2 single car parks per unit; speed ramp every 2 levels.
