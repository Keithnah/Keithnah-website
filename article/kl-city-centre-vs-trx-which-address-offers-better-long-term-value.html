KL City Centre vs TRX: Which Address Offers Better Long-Term Value?

10 Jul 2026 · Keith Nah

KL City Centre and TRX are often mentioned in the same breath, but they serve slightly different buyers and reward slightly different strategies. KL City Centre is the established address — decades of brand recognition, direct views of the Petronas Towers, and a deep resale market. TRX is the newer story: a purpose-built financial district with fresh infrastructure and a smaller, more curated supply of residences.

The Case For KL City Centre: Proven Liquidity

KL City Centre's biggest advantage isn't the skyline — it's liquidity. Because the district has decades of resale history, buyers, agents and banks all have a clear reference point for what a unit should be worth, which makes both buying and selling faster and less uncertain. That matters more than most first-time investors expect, especially if a future sale or refinancing event is part of the plan.

KL City Centre still commands a premium for units with unobstructed tower views, and that premium has proven durable rather than speculative — it reflects a genuinely limited supply of buildings that will ever enjoy that specific vantage point again, since the surrounding plots are largely built out.

The Case For TRX: Capturing The Growth Curve

TRX pricing has been climbing steadily as more corporate tenants move in and the MRT interchange matures into a genuine transit hub rather than a construction site. Rental yields between the two districts are broadly comparable today, though TRX carries an edge with newer buildings, modern M&E systems, and lower near-term maintenance costs — a meaningful consideration over a ten-year hold.

The trade-off is patience. TRX's resale market is still thin simply because the district is young; there isn't yet the deep multi-decade transaction history that KL City Centre has. Investors who are comfortable being early to a district that's still filling out its retail, F&B and corporate tenant mix tend to be the ones best suited to TRX.

How We Advise Clients To Choose

For a long-term hold prioritising stability and ease of eventual resale, KL City Centre remains the safer, more liquid choice. For investors comfortable with a five-to-ten-year horizon who want to capture a district's growth curve as it fills out, TRX offers a different kind of upside — one tied to the district maturing rather than to an already-established address.

In practice, many of our clients end up doing both over time: an established KL City Centre unit for stability and easy liquidity, paired with a TRX unit specifically to capture growth as the district matures. Neither approach is wrong — the right one depends on your time horizon and how much you value liquidity today versus growth potential over the next decade.

Key takeawayThere's no universally "better" answer between KL City Centre and TRX — only a better answer for your specific goals. If liquidity and a proven track record matter most, KL City Centre wins. If you're investing for the next cycle rather than this one, TRX's growth curve is worth the extra patience.
