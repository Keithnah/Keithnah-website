First-time buyer exemption applied to both duties.
Foreign buyers pay a flat 8% on residential property.
First-time buyer exemption does not apply: it needs a Malaysian citizen buying a residential property priced at MYR 500,000 or less.

Citizens and PRs pay MOT duty of 1% on the first MYR 100,000, 2% up to MYR 500,000, 3% up to MYR 1,000,000 and 4% above that. Loan agreement duty is 0.5% of the loan. Legal fees, valuation fees and state consent fees are not included. Rates and exemptions change, so please confirm with Keith or your lawyer.
