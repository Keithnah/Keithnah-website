What is this?

Real Property Gains Tax (RPGT) is a tax on the profit when you sell a property. The rate depends on how long you held it and whether you are a citizen/PR or a foreigner. Selling after the 5th year is much cheaper, so timing matters for investors.

What is this?

Fire insurance covers damage to the building and is normally required by the bank. MRTA (decreasing cover) and MLTA (fixed cover) pay off or help with your home loan if you pass away or become permanently disabled. They are usually optional, but many banks recommend them.

MRTA (Mortgage Reducing Term Assurance)

Cover reduces over time, in line with your outstanding loan. It is usually paid as a single premium upfront, which can be added to the loan. If you pass away or become totally and permanently disabled, the insurer pays the bank to settle the balance. It is the cheaper option, but it only protects the loan and is tied to that loan.

MLTA (Mortgage Level Term Assurance)

Cover stays at the same amount for the whole term, and the premium is usually paid yearly. The payout goes to your family or nominee, who can use it to settle the loan and keep the rest. It costs more than MRTA, but it is more flexible and can still protect your family if you change loans.

Both are usually optional. Compare quotes, because cover, exclusions and premiums differ by insurer.
