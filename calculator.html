Gross yield is yearly rent divided by price. Net yield deducts vacancy and yearly costs. Income tax and agent fees are not included.

What is this?

Rental yield tells you how much rent a property earns each year compared with its price. Gross yield ignores costs; net yield removes vacancy and running costs. Cash flow shows whether the rent covers your monthly instalment.

Uses the individual rates: citizens and PRs pay 30% in years 1 to 3, 20% in year 4, 15% in year 5 and 0% from year 6; foreigners pay 30% up to year 5 and 10% from year 6. The once-in-a-lifetime home exemption and other exemptions are not applied. Please confirm with a tax adviser.

What is this?

Real Property Gains Tax (RPGT) is a tax on the profit when you sell a property. The rate depends on how long you held it and whether you are a citizen/PR or a foreigner. Selling after the 5th year is much cheaper, so timing matters for investors.

What is this?

Fire insurance covers damage to the building and is normally required by the bank. MRTA (decreasing cover) and MLTA (fixed cover) pay off or help with your home loan if you pass away or become permanently disabled. They are usually optional, but many banks recommend them.
