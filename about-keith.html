The Approach

Content-Led, Client-First

Keith built his practice around a simple idea: buyers make better decisions when they can actually see what they're buying. Rather than relying on glossy brochures alone, he produces drone footage, physical site walkthroughs and detailed pricing breakdowns for every project he features — the same videos that built his YouTube following.

That content-first approach means clients often arrive already well-informed, ready to go deeper into financing options, unit comparisons and paperwork specific to their situation — whether they're a first-time foreign buyer or a seasoned investor adding to a portfolio.

Keith works directly with developers to secure early access to new launches, and supports clients through the full journey — from first video, to viewing, to signing, to key collection.
